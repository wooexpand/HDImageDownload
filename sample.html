How do max bids work?

Max Bids

When you have submitted a max bid, we will automatically bid for you, until you have the highest bid or your max bid is reached.

If your max bid is reached and someone places a higher bid, we will notify you with an email.

Other bidders are not able to see how high your max bid is.
